FRIA STUDENT ACCOUNTS — APP VERSIONS
====================================

THE ONLY FILE YOU NEED IS:   index.html      (this is v4.1, the newest)

To put it live:
  1. Go to github.com/stacy-coder/FRIA
  2. Add file  ->  Upload files
  3. Drag index.html in
  4. Scroll down and click the green "Commit changes"
  5. Wait a minute, then hard refresh stacy-coder.github.io/FRIA
     The footer should read v4.1

Then, in the app:
  Settings -> Import Term Bills -> choose FRIA-bills-2026-2027-Term1.json
  Pick Term 1. It shows you what it will do before it writes anything.
  257 bills, GHS 235,191.43.

  Settings -> Bus Fee Rates -> check it reads 10 per day.

WHAT CHANGED, NEWEST FIRST
--------------------------
v4.1  Import a whole term's bills from your class workbooks.
      New fee lines so a bill on screen reads like the paper bill:
      PTA (Annual Package), PTA Non Attendance Fee, Beacon of Light,
      Missing Library Book, BECE Expense, Saturday Classes.
      Scholarships stored as a percentage off full tuition, so the reason stays visible.

v4.0  Arrears and extra charges can actually be paid. Before this, they were added
      to what a student owed but no payment could ever be applied to them, so those
      bills could never reach zero.
      Arrears can now be typed in, corrected and cleared. Carry Forward no longer
      doubles the debt when clicked twice.

v3.9  Bus fees charged by the day at GHS 10, not by the month.
      Day 10, week 50, month 200 or 180 paid in advance, term 700.

v3.8  Sibling, early payment and promo discounts come off School Tuition only,
      not all twelve fee items. A 10% sibling discount was taking GHS 161.50
      instead of GHS 100.
      The Add Extra Charge panel is visible on the account instead of buried
      inside the collapsed fee breakdown.

v3.7  TOTAL ARREARS on the bus sheets no longer shrinks when you narrow the
      "Show Weeks" view. It was quietly dropping weeks out of the totals.

v3.6  Bus fee payments and rates save and reload properly.
      Bus Fee Rates added to Settings. Bus Fees panel added to each rider's account.


THINGS WORTH CHECKING ONCE IT IS UP
-----------------------------------
1. Arrears figures. The Carry Forward button was doubling silently before v4.0,
   so any unusually large arrears figure is worth a second look. You can correct
   them directly on the student's account now.

2. Balances will rise for students on a sibling or scholarship discount, because
   the discount now comes off tuition only. That is the correction landing, not a
   new fault. Worth warning whoever answers the phone.

3. The bill importer will also move students into the class shown on their bill,
   which promotes the whole school into 2026-2027. There is a checkbox if you
   would rather do that separately.
